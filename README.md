### 🧬 GDM Early Prediction & Explainable AI
**Python · Scikit-learn · XGBoost · LightGBM · CatBoost · SHAP · Gemini API**

[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/aanyaagarwal1/GDM-Early-Prediction-Explainable-AI) · [Research Paper](https://github.com/aanyaagarwal1/GDM-Early-Prediction-Explainable-AI/blob/main/paper/research_paper.pdf)

An explainable machine learning framework for early Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM) risk estimation without OGTT, integrated with LLM-assisted clinical report analysis.

- Developed and benchmarked six classifiers: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Extra Trees, XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost.
- Applied stratified train-test splitting, randomized hyperparameter search, five-fold cross-validation, and SMOTE-based class balancing.
- Achieved **97.31% accuracy, 96.59% F1-score, and 0.9979 ROC-AUC** using Extra Trees in the reported OGTT-free experiment.
- Applied SHAP to interpret model predictions and identify influential clinical features.
- Integrated Gemini-based extraction of structured clinical information from PDF and image reports.

**Research Publication:** Co-authored research accepted at the 8th International Conference on Soft Computing and its Engineering Applications (**icSoftComp2026**).
